About

Plumage encrypts your message (AES-256-GCM, key stretched from your pass-phrase), splits it, and hides each shard inside ordinary-looking media. Everything happens on your device — nothing is uploaded. This is privacy tooling for your own communications; use it lawfully. Plain LSB can be detected by steganalysis — treat it as concealment, with the encryption as the real protection.
